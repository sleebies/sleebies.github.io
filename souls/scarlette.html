Scarlette is a highly advanced android for her time, capable of critical outside the box thinking, an extremely well crafted and durable body, an organic artificial intelligence, capable of understanding and learning overtime.

When created, she seemed to imprint on the first real skill she ever learned, which was how to fight. This led to her being mostly capable of all forms of combat, however what she is most famous for is her swordsmanship. She has lost very few fights, and has always been able to perform what she calls "a tactical retreat" when she needs to. There is no other sword fighter like her.

weapon

A sword made out of a mysterious metal. It's essentially a rapier that has the ability to slash and cut just as easily as it can stab. It also does not bend like one, it stays completely rigid.

sidearm

A modified single action army revolver. It can fire 8 shots before needing to reload. Her ammo is generated "in-house", which means her body creates a collection of 8 mysterious bullets for her to fire.
